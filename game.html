Before you get on a real slope

Fall Line — the ski simulator

A browser game built for this trip: an endless alpine descent where turn shape is your brake, exactly as it is on snow. It won't teach you to ski — nothing but snow does that — but it puts edges, turn shape and speed control in your head before you're standing at the top of a real one.

⛷️ Fall Line Needs a keyboard, so this one is for laptops and desktops. Sound is generated live as you ski — headphones recommended. Play the game

Press SPACE to drop in — the game takes the keyboard as soon as it loads. If the keys stop responding, click the game once to give it back. Full screen in a new tab ↗

The controls

Two hands: one steers, one decides how hard you're working. The effort keys are the whole game — the game explains all of this on its title screen too.

What it's for

The one idea worth arriving with: you slow down by turning across the hill, not by leaning back and hoping. The game is unforgiving about it, which is the point.

🧑‍🏫

What it isn't

A substitute for a lesson. A real instructor for a real half-day is included in the trip package, and that's what actually gets you down a slope.

⛷️

Then do it properly

Beginner group lessons are built into the itinerary on the first morning at Shymbulak, with gear hire covered.
