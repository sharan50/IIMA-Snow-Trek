Before you get on a real slope

The ski simulator

A little browser game built for this trip. It won't teach you to ski — nothing but snow does that — but it gets the basic idea of edges, turns and speed into your head before you're standing at the top of one.

🎯

What it's for

Getting the mechanics into your head — turning, controlling speed, picking a line down the hill — so day one at Shymbulak isn't the very first time you've thought about any of it.

🧑‍🏫

What it isn't

A substitute for a lesson. A real instructor for a real half-day is included in the trip package, and that's what actually gets you down a slope.

⛷️

Then do it properly

Beginner group lessons are built into the itinerary on the first morning at Shymbulak, with gear hire covered.
