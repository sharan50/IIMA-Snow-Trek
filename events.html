The other half of the trip

Networking & Mixer Events

The Snow Trek isn't just about the skiing — every edition is built around dedicated moments to reconnect across batches, campuses, and years.

1
Evening

Welcome Reception & Icebreaker Mixer

The trip kicks off with a hosted dinner and casual mixer on arrival night. Quick trip briefing, batch and campus introductions, and structured icebreakers so first-timers and different IIMA campuses mix from day one rather than sticking to their own group chats.

All attendeesHosted dinner
2
Evening

Apres-Ski Mixer

A relaxed evening back in Almaty after the first full day at Shymbulak — drinks, mulled wine, and easy conversation comparing notes on the day's runs. Lower-key than the gala, aimed at informal, small-group catch-ups.

All attendeesCasual

Evening

Alumni Gala Dinner & Fireside Chat

The trip's marquee event: a sit-down dinner followed by a fireside conversation with alumni founders, operators, or investors from the group — a chance to swap notes on what people are building and working on, in a setting that's built for it.

Why we do this

Networking that doesn't feel like networking

Every mixer is designed around a simple idea: put people from different campuses, batches, and industries in the same room, on a shared high after a day on the mountain, and let the conversations happen naturally.

🎓

Cross-batch by design

Seating, icebreakers, and group activities are deliberately mixed across IIMA batches and campuses.

💼

Real career conversations

The fireside chat and gala dinner are built to surface what people are actually working on.

🔁

A tradition, not a one-off

Edition I built the first version of this alumni network on snow — Edition II keeps it going.
