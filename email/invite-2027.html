Why Kazakhstan

Two reasons this destination won

We looked hard at the Alps and at Japan. Kazakhstan beat both on the two things that actually decide whether a group of thirty people makes it onto a mountain together.

Reason one

Visa-free for Indian passports

No application. No embassy appointment. No biometrics, no fee, no three-week wait with your passport sitting in someone else's drawer. Indian passport holders enter Kazakhstan visa-free for stays of up to 14 days — comfortably more than the five days this trip needs. You book a flight and you go.

If you have ever tried to get thirty people Schengen appointments in January, you already understand why this mattered more than anything else on the list.

Reason two

Some of the best skiing outside the Alps and the Rockies

Shymbulak is not a novelty. It is a full-scale, modern resort that hosted the Asian Winter Games, and it sits 25 minutes from a city of two million people:

• Base at 2,260 m, lifts to 3,200 m at Talgar Pass — roughly a kilometre of vertical, on dry continental snow
• Modern gondolas straight up from Medeu, English-speaking instruction, full rental fleet — everything a first-timer needs and enough mountain for anyone who isn't
• A day pass runs about ₹2,250 and gear hire about ₹1,050 a day — a fraction of Alpine or Colorado pricing
• No punishing transfer at the far end: sleep in the city, ski the mountain, be back for dinner

And the part nobody expects: Almaty is roughly a three-hour flight from Delhi. Closer than most of Europe, cheaper than all of it, and a genuinely good city — Soviet bones, Silk Road appetite, mountains at the end of every street.

4 nights, 4 full days

The trip at a glance

FRI 22 JAN Arrival. Land in Almaty, settle in, and meet everyone properly over a hosted welcome dinner.
SAT 23 JAN First day on snow. Up to Shymbulak for lessons or free riding, then mulled wine back in the city to compare notes.
SUN 24 JAN Second day on snow. More mountain if you want it, tubing and snowshoeing if you don't, and an evening left deliberately free.
MON 25 JAN Almaty, off the mountain. Kok Tobe, the Green Bazaar, skating at Medeu, and the big group dinner of the trip.
TUE 26 JAN Last morning & home. A third ski day or Big Almaty Lake, farewell brunch, then flights out on Republic Day itself.
