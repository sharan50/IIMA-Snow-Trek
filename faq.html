Before you go

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visas, weather, currency, and getting ready for Almaty and Shymbulak.

Accordion
Visa requirements depend on your nationality and passport — Kazakhstan offers visa-free entry and e-Visa options for many countries. Please check the current requirements for your specific passport on Kazakhstan's official e-Visa portal well ahead of the trip, since rules can change. We'll share nationality-specific guidance closer to the date once the group list is finalized.
Almaty in January typically sees temperatures around -5°C to -10°C, with the mountains at Shymbulak running colder, especially higher up. Expect snow, so pack accordingly — see the packing question below.
Thermal base layers, a waterproof/windproof ski jacket and pants, warm gloves, a hat/beanie, ski goggles or sunglasses, warm waterproof boots, and wool socks. If you don't own ski gear, most of it can be rented at Shymbulak — see the Activities page for what's included in the trip package.
The local currency is the Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT). Cards are widely accepted in Almaty, but it's worth carrying some cash for markets like the Green Bazaar and smaller vendors on the mountain.
Not at all. Shymbulak has beginner-friendly slopes and group lessons are included for first-timers. If skiing isn't for you, there's plenty to do on the mountain and in Almaty — see the Activities page for non-snow options.
Travel insurance is not included in the trip price but is strongly recommended, particularly given the skiing and snowboarding component. Please arrange your own policy that covers winter sports before the trip.
The nearest airport is Almaty International Airport (ALA), served by connections from most major hubs. International flights are not included in the trip price — see the Pricing page for estimated round-trip fares from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Singapore, and London for the Jan 22–26, 2027 window. Book your own once dates are confirmed, and we'll share suggested arrival windows so transfers can be coordinated.
Depends what you pick at registration. "Single — Grouped Stay" means a shared 4-bed hostel dorm; the "Single Stay" options give you a private room instead (economic or luxury). Family archetypes assume a family sharing one room. You can request a specific roommate/dorm-mate in the registration form, or we'll pair you up — see the Pricing page for what each option costs.
A deposit secures your spot, with the balance due closer to the trip once headcount and vendor rates are finalized — see the Pricing page for the payment schedule. Full cancellation terms will be shared with your registration confirmation.
Reach out to the trip organizer at p24dhruv@iima.ac.in, or leave a note in the comments field when you register your interest.
