Snow + City

Things to do

Up to three full days on the mountain at Shymbulak, plus a full non-snow day around Almaty for rest days, non-skiers, and anyone who wants a break from the cold.

On the mountain at Shymbulak

Kazakhstan's best-known ski resort, ~25 minutes from central Almaty and reachable by gondola — with terrain and options for every level.

🎿

Alpine skiing All levels

Groomed runs from beginner-friendly slopes near the base to longer intermediate and advanced descents higher up the mountain.

🏂

Snowboarding All levels

Dedicated boarder-friendly runs and a terrain park for anyone wanting to spend the trip on one board instead of two skis.

🧑‍🏫

Group lessons for beginners Beginner

Half-day group instruction for first-timers — no prior experience needed, gear rental included.

Cable car to Talgar Pass

A scenic gondola ride up to ~3,163m for panoramic views of the Tien Shan range — worth doing even if you're not skiing that day.

🛷

Snow tubing & sledding No experience needed

A lower-key, high-fun option for non-skiers or anyone wanting an easy break between runs.

🥾

Snowshoeing

Guided snowshoe walks off the main runs for a quieter way to take in the mountain scenery.

Ice skating at Medeu

The world's highest-altitude skating rink sits on the way up to Shymbulak — an easy add-on for an afternoon.

🚁

Heli-skiing Optional add-on

For experienced skiers wanting untouched backcountry powder — bookable separately at extra cost, subject to interest and weather.

☕

Apres-ski lounges

Mountain cafes and lounges for mulled wine, hot chocolate, and warming up between runs.

Around Almaty

Kazakhstan's cultural capital — a green, walkable city with Soviet-era landmarks, markets, and mountain views from nearly every street.

Kok Tobe Hill

Cable car up to Almaty's signature viewpoint, with a small amusement park and panoramic views over the whole city.

Zenkov Cathedral & Panfilov Park

One of the world's tallest wooden buildings, built without nails, set inside a leafy central park.

Green Bazaar

Almaty's largest indoor market — local produce, dried fruit, spices, and street food in one place.

🕌

Central Mosque & Arbat (Zhibek Zholy)

A short walk through the city's pedestrian shopping street, cafes, and street performers.

Big Almaty Lake

A half-day excursion to a turquoise alpine lake in the mountains just outside the city — weather and road access permitting.

🍲

Local food tour

Beshbarmak, plov, and other Kazakh and Central Asian dishes, guided through a few local favorites.

🧖

Banya (traditional bathhouse)

An optional recovery session for tired legs after a day on the slopes.

🖼️

Museums

The Central State Museum and the Museum of Kazakh Musical Instruments for a dose of local history and culture.

🛒

Shopping

Dostyk Plaza and Mega Almaty for anyone looking to shop between activities.
