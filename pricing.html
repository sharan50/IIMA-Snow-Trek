Pricing

Preliminary per-person estimates for the 4-night, 4-full-day trip over the Republic Day long weekend — Friday, January 22 to Tuesday, January 26, 2027 — across five accommodation archetypes, from solo economy to family luxury, plus flights and everyday spending money. Final pricing is confirmed once headcount is locked in.

These are planning estimates, not final prices. Actual per-person cost depends on final group size, hotel and airfare rates at time of booking, and exchange rates. Numbers below will be updated once venue and transfer contracts are confirmed — treat them as a ballpark for budgeting purposes.

Every fare above was re-selected end to end so the arrival actually fits, across the dated itineraries we checked through to the booking step. Second, none of these totals includes checked baggage, and London's Light fare does not include a cabin bag either — read the note beside each fare before comparing them with each other. Third, Bengaluru is deliberately blank.     Good to know

    Payment & policies

    1️⃣

    Reserve with a deposit

    A deposit will secure your spot and help lock in group rates on hotels and lift passes. The amount, the date it stays refundable until, and the cancellation terms are all still to be set — treat anything you have heard about them as provisional until we publish them here.

    2️⃣

    Balance due closer to travel

    The remaining balance is due once final headcount, hotel, and transfer rates are confirmed — provisionally 4–6 weeks before departure, though that schedule is not fixed yet either.

    3️⃣

    Group size & capacity

    The trip is capped at roughly 30 alumni to keep group logistics and lessons feeling personal. Spots are held on a first-registered basis.
