✅ What's included

  • 3 nights accommodation (twin-sharing) in Almaty
  • Return airport transfers within Almaty
  • Daily transfers to/from Shymbulak
  • 2-day Shymbulak lift pass
  • Ski/snowboard equipment rental
  • Beginner group lessons (one session)
  • Daily breakfast
  • Welcome reception, apres-ski mixer, gala dinner, and farewell brunch
  • On-ground trip coordinator & WhatsApp support group

❌ What's not included

  • International flights to/from Almaty (ALA)
  • Kazakhstan visa fees, where applicable
  • Travel & medical insurance (strongly recommended)
  • Lunches and dinners outside hosted events
  • Personal ski/snowboard gear purchases
  • Optional add-ons: heli-skiing, banya, spa treatments
  • Alcohol beyond hosted mixer/dinner events
  • Personal shopping and incidental expenses
Good to know

Payment & policies

1️⃣

Reserve with a deposit

A refundable-until-a-set-date deposit secures your spot and helps lock in group rates on hotels and lift passes.

2️⃣

Balance due closer to travel

The remaining balance is due once final headcount, hotel, and transfer rates are confirmed — typically 4-6 weeks before departure.

3️⃣

Group size & capacity

The trip is capped at roughly 30 alumni to keep group logistics and the mixer events feeling personal. Spots are held on a first-registered basis.
