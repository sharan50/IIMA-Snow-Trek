Pricing

Preliminary per-person estimates for the 4-night, 4-full-day trip over the Republic Day long weekend — Friday, January 22 to Tuesday, January 26, 2027 — across five accommodation archetypes, from solo economy to family luxury, plus flights and everyday spending money. Land package · by accommodation type

What it costs, per person

Built up from real component costs — Almaty hotel rates by category, Shymbulak lift passes & rental, shared transfers, and event costs — checked September 2026. Every archetype includes the same $320pp of shared inclusions (breakdown below); only the room changes.

Accommodation archetype Room basis Per person (4 nights) Approx. INR
Single Stay — Economic Private room, 2–3★ hotel (~$45/night) $460 – $540 ₹39,000 – ₹45,000
Single Stay — Luxury Private room, 5★ hotel (~$320/night) $1,480 – $1,750 ₹1,24,000 – ₹1,47,000
Single — Grouped Stay 4-bed hostel dorm, shared with other alumni (~$12/night/bed) $340 – $400 ₹29,000 – ₹34,000
Family — Economic Family of 4 in one economy family room, ~$120/night, split 4 ways $400 – $480 ₹34,000 – ₹40,000
Family — Luxury Family of 4 in one luxury suite, ~$425/night, split 4 ways $680 – $810 ₹57,000 – ₹68,000

Each figure = ~$320 in shared package inclusions per person (shared airport transfer $20 + 3 days of Shymbulak transfers $38 + 3-day lift pass $64 + 3-day gear rental $45 + one group lesson $25 + the 4 hosted events $85 + on-ground coordinator $45) + that archetype's accommodation share for 4 nights. "Grouped Stay" means a shared 4-bed dorm room, not a private twin-share — Almaty has a handful of hostels that can do this (e.g. Almaty Backpackers, Hostel Nomad), but a group of ~30 will likely need to book across 2-3 properties or well in advance; if dorm space runs out by the time we book, this archetype falls back to twin-sharing a standard hotel room (~3★, ~$85/night split 2 ways → roughly $505-575pp). "Family" assumes 4 people sharing one room — a family of 3 will land slightly higher per person; a non-skiing child removes roughly $130pp (their share of lift pass + rental + lesson across the 3 ski days). An earlier version of this table used an unrealistically cheap $55/night rate for the "economy family room," which undercounted that archetype relative to a real quad-occupancy room (~$120/night is closer to what Almaty family rooms actually go for, well below the ~$212/night city-wide average and the $425+ luxury suites). These are cross-checked planning figures, not contracted group rates — real quotes for a group booking this size could move them ±15% either way, and we'll firm them up once venues are confirmed.

Everyday spending · not included in the package

Food, drink & other out-of-pocket costs

Breakfast is covered by your room, and the Welcome Reception, Gala Dinner, and Farewell Brunch are full hosted meals — but the Apres-Ski Mixer is drinks/snacks only and Sunday has no hosted event at all. This is what's left: every lunch, those 2 missing dinners, snacks, and a few small extras.

Item Basis Per person, whole trip
Lunches (4 full days) ~$9 casual meal in Almaty, ~$13 at Shymbulak (prices run 30-50% higher on the mountain) $40 – $50
Saturday & Sunday dinners Mid-range Almaty restaurant, ~$21pp each. Neither night has a full hosted meal — the Apres-Ski Mixer is drinks/snacks only, and Sunday is a free evening $36 – $46
Snacks, coffee, water ~$5/day across the 5-day trip $20 – $28
Alcohol / bar tabs beyond hosted events Highly variable — a few drinks out $0 – $40

Total food & drink beyond the package: roughly $95 – $165 per person (~₹8,000 – ₹13,800) for the whole trip — grounded in Numbeo's Almaty cost-of-living data (~£9 for an inexpensive meal, ~£33/~$42 for a mid-range three-course meal for two) and Shymbulak's own published on-mountain food prices (2,500-5,000 KZT per meal). Double-check against the itinerary: of the 4 nights, only 2 (Welcome Reception, Gala Dinner) come with a full hosted meal — the Apres-Ski Mixer is drinks/snacks only and Sunday has no hosted event, so those 2 dinners are on you, which is what pushed this estimate up from an earlier draft that only budgeted for one of them. This also excludes souvenirs/shopping, spa or banya add-ons, tips, and a Kazakhstan e-Visa fee where applicable (see the FAQ) — budget a bit extra on top for those.

✅ What's included

  • 4 nights accommodation in Almaty, in the archetype you choose
  • Return airport transfers (shared shuttle)
  • Transfers to/from Shymbulak on ski days (shared shuttle)
  • Up to 3-day Shymbulak lift pass
  • Ski/snowboard equipment rental (up to 3 days)
  • Beginner group lessons (one session)
  • Daily breakfast
  • Welcome reception, apres-ski mixer, gala dinner, and farewell brunch
  • On-ground trip coordinator & WhatsApp support group

❌ What's not included

  • International flights to/from Almaty (ALA)
  • Kazakhstan visa fees, where applicable
  • Travel & medical insurance (strongly recommended)
  • Lunches and dinners outside hosted events (see estimate above)
  • Personal ski/snowboard gear purchases
  • Optional add-ons: heli-skiing, banya, spa treatments
  • Alcohol beyond hosted mixer/dinner events
  • Personal shopping and incidental expenses
Good to know

Payment & policies

1️⃣

Reserve with a deposit

A refundable-until-a-set-date deposit secures your spot and helps lock in group rates on hotels and lift passes.

2️⃣

Balance due closer to travel

The remaining balance is due once final headcount, hotel, and transfer rates are confirmed — typically 4-6 weeks before departure.

3️⃣

Group size & capacity

The trip is capped at roughly 30 alumni to keep group logistics and the mixer events feeling personal. Spots are held on a first-registered basis.
