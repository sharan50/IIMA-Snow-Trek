    Where it goes Line item Cost

    These are book-early numbers. Every figure the calculator uses sits at roughly the 25th percentile of what we found on the market rather than the midpoint — the price you get if you book in good time and shop around, not the average. Leave it late and expect the upper half of the ranges published above instead. Two things carry no percentile because they're fixed published prices: Shymbulak's day passes (9,000 KZT weekday, 12,000 KZT weekend, 3,000 KZT for ages 6–10 — our Saturday and Sunday are the pricier weekend days, though the New Year surcharge window has closed by our dates) and the ticketed extras, a banya visit at 10,000 KZT (~₹2,000) and Medeu at 1,800 KZT entry plus 1,000 KZT skate hire (~₹550). Gear hire assumes renting in Almaty (~5,000 KZT/day, ~₹1,050) rather than on the mountain (~7,000 KZT). Costs are sourced in dollars and tenge and converted for display at ₹95 to the dollar (the rate on 8 September 2026) and roughly 470 KZT to the dollar, so rupee figures move with the exchange rate. The hosted meals and the coordination fee are our own numbers rather than quoted rates. Rooms are booked to capacity, so a party of four in a 2-person room is billed as two rooms. Heli-skiing is deliberately absent: it's quoted trip by trip, so ask rather than guess.

    Land package · by accommodation type

    What it costs, per person

    Built up from real component costs — Almaty hotel rates by category, Shymbulak lift passes & rental, shared transfers, and event costs — checked September 2026. Every archetype includes the same ~₹24,700pp of shared inclusions (breakdown below); only the room changes. Most people take a comfortable private room; the dorm option is there for anyone who genuinely prefers it.

    Accommodation archetype Room basis Per person (4 nights) Approx. USD
    Single Stay — Luxury Private room, 5★ hotel (~₹27,100/night) ₹1,22,500 – ₹1,46,500 $1,290 – $1,540
    Family — Luxury Family of 4 in one luxury suite, ~₹33,250/night, split 4 ways ₹53,000 – ₹63,500 $560 – $670
    Single Stay — Economic Private room, 2–3★ hotel (~₹3,600/night) ₹36,000 – ₹43,000 $380 – $450
    Family — Economic Family of 4 in one economy family room, ~₹10,000/night, split 4 ways ₹32,500 – ₹38,000 $340 – $400
    Single — Grouped Stay 4-bed hostel dorm, shared with other alumni (~₹850/night/bed) ₹25,500 – ₹31,500 $270 – $330

    Each figure = ~₹24,700 in shared package inclusions per person (shared airport transfer ₹1,150 + 3 days of Shymbulak transfers ₹2,550 + 3-day lift pass ₹6,750 + 3-day gear rental ₹3,150 + one group lesson ₹2,100 + the 4 hosted dinners/hangouts ₹6,200 + on-ground coordinator ₹2,850) + that archetype's accommodation share for 4 nights. These sit at roughly the 25th percentile of market rates rather than the midpoint, so they assume booking early and shopping around. The lift-pass line is the one that cannot be shopped down: it is a published rate, and two of our three ski days fall on a weekend at 12,000 KZT rather than the 9,000 KZT weekday price. "Grouped Stay" means a shared 4-bed dorm room, not a private twin-share — Almaty has a handful of hostels that can do this (e.g. Almaty Backpackers, Hostel Nomad), but a group of ~30 will likely need to book across 2-3 properties or well in advance; if dorm space runs out by the time we book, this archetype falls back to twin-sharing a standard hotel room (~3★, ~₹6,650/night split 2 ways → roughly ₹35,000-42,000pp). "Family" assumes 4 people sharing one room — a family of 3 will land slightly higher per person; a non-skiing child removes roughly ₹11,000pp (their share of lift pass + rental + lesson across the 3 ski days). The economy family room sits at ~₹10,000/night — a real quad-occupancy room booked early, against a ~₹20,000/night city-wide average for family rooms and ₹33,000+ for luxury suites. These are cross-checked planning figures, not contracted group rates — real quotes for a group booking this size could move them ±15% either way, and we'll firm them up once venues are confirmed.

    Everyday spending · not included in the package

    Food, drink & other out-of-pocket costs

    Breakfast is covered by your room, and the Welcome Dinner, Group Dinner Night, and Farewell Brunch are full hosted meals — but the Apres-Ski Hangout is drinks/snacks only and Sunday has no hosted event at all. This is what's left: every lunch, those 2 missing dinners, snacks, and a few small extras.

    Item Basis Per person, whole trip Approx. USD
    Lunches (4 full days) ~₹650 casual meal in Almaty, ~₹1,050 at Shymbulak (prices run 30-50% higher on the mountain) ₹3,000 – ₹4,000 $32 – $42
    Saturday & Sunday dinners Mid-range Almaty restaurant, ~₹1,600pp each. Neither night has a full hosted meal — the Apres-Ski Hangout is drinks/snacks only, and Sunday is a free evening ₹2,800 – ₹3,800 $30 – $40
    Snacks, coffee, water ~₹400/day across the 5-day trip ₹1,500 – ₹2,300 $16 – $24
    Alcohol / bar tabs beyond hosted events Highly variable — a few drinks out ₹0 – ₹3,800 $0 – $40

    Total food & drink beyond the package: roughly ₹7,600 – ₹13,800 per person (~$80 – $145) for the whole trip — grounded in Numbeo's Almaty cost-of-living data (~£9 for an inexpensive meal, ~£33/~$42 for a mid-range three-course meal for two) and Shymbulak's own published on-mountain food prices (2,500-5,000 KZT per meal). Double-check against the itinerary: of the 4 nights, only 2 (Welcome Dinner, Group Dinner Night) come with a full hosted meal — the Apres-Ski Hangout is drinks/snacks only and Sunday has no hosted event, so those 2 dinners are on you, which is what pushed this estimate up from an earlier draft that only budgeted for one of them. This also excludes souvenirs/shopping, spa or banya add-ons, tips, and a Kazakhstan e-Visa fee where applicable (see the FAQ) — budget a bit extra on top for those.

    ✅ What's included

    • 4 nights accommodation in Almaty, in the archetype you choose
    • Return airport transfers (shared shuttle)
    • Transfers to/from Shymbulak on ski days (shared shuttle)
    • Up to 3-day Shymbulak lift pass
    • Ski/snowboard equipment rental (up to 3 days)
    • Beginner group lessons (one session)
    • Daily breakfast
    • Welcome dinner, apres-ski hangout, group dinner night, and farewell brunch
    • On-ground trip coordinator & WhatsApp support group

    ❌ What's not included

    • International flights to/from Almaty (ALA)
    • Kazakhstan visa fees, where applicable
    • Travel & medical insurance (strongly recommended)
    • Lunches and dinners outside hosted events (see estimate above)
    • Personal ski/snowboard gear purchases
    • Optional add-ons: heli-skiing, banya, spa treatments
    • Alcohol beyond hosted dinner/hangout events
    • Personal shopping and incidental expenses
    Good to know

    Payment & policies

    1️⃣

    Reserve with a deposit

    A refundable-until-a-set-date deposit secures your spot and helps lock in group rates on hotels and lift passes.

    2️⃣

    Balance due closer to travel

    The remaining balance is due once final headcount, hotel, and transfer rates are confirmed — typically 4-6 weeks before departure.

    3️⃣

    Group size & capacity

    The trip is capped at roughly 30 alumni to keep group logistics and lessons feeling personal. Spots are held on a first-registered basis.
