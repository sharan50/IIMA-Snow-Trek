4 Nights · 4 Full Days

Day-by-day itinerary

Based out of Almaty for all four nights, with day trips up the mountain to Shymbulak — no changing hotels, no wasted travel time.

Trip dates: Friday, January 22 – Tuesday, January 26, 2027 (Republic Day long weekend) — 4 nights, 4 full days, anchored on the Jan 26 public holiday. Times and running order are indicative and will be finalized closer to the trip based on flight schedules and group size.
00
Fri, Jan 22 · Arrival
Afternoon

Arrive into Almaty (ALA)

Group and independent arrivals welcomed at the airport; shared transfers to the hotel in central Almaty.

Evening

Check-in & free time

Settle in, rest up from travel, optional short walk around the hotel neighborhood.

Evening

Welcome Reception & Icebreaker Mixer

Trip briefing, batch introductions, and a hosted dinner to kick things off. See the Networking page for details.

01
Sat, Jan 23 · Full Day
Morning

Transfer to Shymbulak

~25 minute drive plus gondola ride up the mountain. Gear rental and group lesson sign-ups for beginners.

All Day

Skiing & snowboarding — Day 1

Full day on the slopes across all skill levels — see Snow Activities for the full list of runs and options.

Evening

Apres-Ski Mixer

Casual drinks and mulled wine back in Almaty, comparing notes on the day's runs.

02
Sun, Jan 24 · Full Day
All Day

Skiing & snowboarding — Day 2

Free ski/ride time for those who want more mountain, plus optional snow tubing and snowshoeing for non-skiers.

Evening

Free evening

No hosted event tonight — rest up, explore Almaty's restaurants on your own, or keep the apres-ski going informally.

03
Mon, Jan 25 · Full Day
All Day

Almaty city day

Kok Tobe, Panfilov Park & Zenkov Cathedral, the Green Bazaar, and a local food tour — see Non-Snow Activities.

Evening

Alumni Gala Dinner & Fireside Chat

The trip's marquee networking event — a sit-down dinner with a guest fireside conversation.

04
Tue, Jan 26 · Full Day & Departure (Republic Day)
Morning

Flex morning: 3rd ski day or Big Almaty Lake

Choose a final morning at Shymbulak (lift pass covers up to 3 days) or a half-day excursion to Big Almaty Lake for non-skiers.

Midday

Farewell Brunch & Group Photo

One last meal together before people start heading to the airport.

Afternoon / Evening

Airport transfers & departure

Shared transfers back to Almaty International Airport. Some flights back to India/Singapore/London may depart Jan 26 evening or the morning of Jan 27 depending on connections — see flight estimates.
