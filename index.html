What is the IIMA Snow Trek?

A once-a-year excuse to get the batch back together — on snow, off campus, and off the group chat.

🏔️

Edition I: The Dolomites

Last year's inaugural Snow Trek took a crew of IIMA alumni to the Italian Dolomites — the trip that started the tradition and set the bar for what "the Snowflake" should feel like.

🇰🇿

Edition II: Kazakhstan

This year we're headed to Central Asia: based out of Almaty with day trips up to Shymbulak, one of the region's best-connected ski resorts — just 25 minutes from the city.

🤝

Built for reconnecting

Every edition blends real skiing and snowboarding time with curated non-snow experiences and dedicated alumni networking evenings — this is a trip, not just a ski holiday.
